Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ViceClips is a personal tool run by the owner of the ViceClips YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner.

What the app accesses

The app signs in to the owner's own Google account using Google OAuth and requests:

  • YouTube upload: to upload clips to the owner's ViceClips channel;
  • YouTube read-only: to confirm which channel it is signed in to and to look up public information about trending videos.

What is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared, sold or sent anywhere other than Google. The app does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone else, and it has no other users.

Use of Google data

ViceClips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the ViceClips YouTube channel's About page.
